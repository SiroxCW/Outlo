The Outlook experience, on Linux

outlook-for-linux is an open source mail client for the Linux desktop. It looks and works like Outlook on Windows, and it is a real desktop app, not a wrapper around the web version.

The project is in early development. There is no release yet. You can follow the progress on GitHub.

View on GitHub

What we are building

  • Microsoft accounts first: outlook.com, hotmail and work or school accounts.
  • Several accounts at once, with one unified inbox.
  • Gmail and every other provider that offers IMAP and SMTP.
  • The layout you know: folder pane, message list, reading pane, ribbon and the same keyboard shortcuts.
  • Works offline, with fast local search across all accounts.
  • Web mode for every Microsoft account: the app shows Outlook on the web and needs no permission to your mailbox. Company and school accounts work this way even where the organization does not allow other mail apps.
  • Packages for Fedora, Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE and Arch, plus a Flatpak.

Your mail stays yours

The app talks directly to your mail provider and keeps your mail on your own computer. We run no servers that see your data. There are no ads and there is no telemetry. The privacy policy has the details.
