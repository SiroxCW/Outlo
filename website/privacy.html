Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The app is still in development. This policy describes how it is built to work. We will update it before the first release if anything changes.

In short

  • Outlo Mail runs on your computer and talks directly to your mail provider.
  • Your mail is stored on your computer. We never receive it.
  • We run no servers that collect data from the app.
  • The app has no telemetry, no analytics and no ads.

Who we are

Outlo Mail is an open source project developed by volunteers. "We" means the maintainers of the project. You can reach us through the issue tracker on GitHub.

What the app accesses

When you add an account, you sign in at your provider (for example Microsoft or Google) or enter the details of your mail server. With your permission the app can then read and change the following data of that account:

  • messages, folders and attachments
  • mailbox settings such as your time zone and automatic replies
  • your name and email address, to label the account in the app
  • calendars and contacts, once you use those parts of the app

The app uses this data for one purpose only: to show your mail to you and to carry out what you do in the app, such as sending, moving or deleting a message.

Where your data is stored

  • The app keeps a copy of your mail in a database in your home directory, so that it works offline and search is fast.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored encrypted with the keyring of your desktop (for example GNOME Keyring or KWallet). If your system has no keyring, the app warns you.
  • When you remove an account from the app, the local copy of its data and its sign-in tokens are deleted from your computer. Your mail at the provider is not touched.

Accounts in web mode

Some organizations do not allow other mail apps to access their mailboxes. For such an account the app can show Outlook on the web instead. In this mode:

  • You use Microsoft's own web app in a separate, isolated part of the app.
  • The app does not copy the mail of that account into its database.
  • The cookies of that sign-in are stored on your computer. They are deleted when you remove the account.
  • For the web app itself, the privacy terms of Microsoft and of your organization apply.

Who receives your data

Nobody except your own mail provider. We have no access to your accounts, your mail or your passwords. We do not sell, share or transfer your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Connections the app makes

  • To the providers of the accounts you added, to sign in and to synchronize your data.
  • To servers named in an email, but only if you allow it. Remote images are blocked by default, because they can be used to track whether you opened a message.
  • To GitHub, to look for a new version. This applies only to the AppImage build. All other builds are updated by your package manager.

Links that you click in an email open in your web browser, not in the app.

Google user data

If you add a Google account, the app accesses your Gmail data only on your computer and only to provide the mail features described above. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit it, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. We set no cookies, use no analytics and load nothing from other servers.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Every earlier version stays available in the history of this file on GitHub.
